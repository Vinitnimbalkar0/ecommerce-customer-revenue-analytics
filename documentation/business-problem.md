# Business Problem

## Project Overview

The objective of this project is to analyze e-commerce transaction data and identify the key factors driving revenue, customer value, product performance, market contribution, and repeat purchasing behavior.

The analysis is designed from a **management decision-making perspective**, rather than simply creating descriptive charts.

## Business Scenario

Management wants to understand:

- How revenue is changing over time
- Which customers contribute the most revenue
- How concentrated revenue is among customers
- Which products drive revenue
- Which markets contribute the most revenue
- How important repeat customers are
- Which customers should receive different engagement strategies
- When customers return after their first purchase
- Which products may have cross-selling opportunities

The goal is to translate transactional data into **actionable business insights and recommendations**.

## Primary Executive Question

> **Which customers, products, and markets are driving revenue, and where are the biggest opportunities to improve customer value and revenue?**

## Executive Questions

| ID | Executive Question | Decision Focus | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | How has monthly revenue changed over the analysis period, and which periods show meaningful growth or decline? | Identify growth/decline periods | Medium |
| Q2 | Which products drive revenue? | Product prioritization | High |
| Q3 | Which markets generate the most revenue? | Market focus | High |
| Q4 | Who are our highest-value customers? | Customer prioritization | High |
| Q5 | How concentrated is revenue among customers? | Customer risk | High |
| Q6 | What proportion of customers are repeat customers? | Retention strategy | High |
| Q7 | Which customers are most valuable based on RFM? | Targeted engagement | High |
| Q8 | When do customers return after their first purchase? | Cohort retention | High |
| Q9 | Which products are purchased together? | Cross-sell opportunities | Medium |

## Expected Business Outcomes

The analysis aims to help management:

1. Understand revenue performance.
2. Identify high-value customers.
3. Reduce customer concentration risk.
4. Improve repeat purchasing.
5. Prioritize products based on evidence.
6. Understand geographic revenue dependence.
7. Identify potential market opportunities.
8. Develop customer-specific engagement strategies.
9. Identify potential cross-selling opportunities.

## Scope

The analysis covers:

- Revenue
- Orders
- Units sold
- Customers
- Products
- Markets
- Customer concentration
- Repeat purchasing
- RFM segmentation
- Cohort retention
- Product basket analysis

## Out of Scope

The dataset does not contain the information required to directly analyze:

- Profit
- Profit margin
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- Marketing ROI
- Inventory turnover
- Market share

These metrics are therefore not claimed in the analysis.
